about our website

From sizzling tibs and rich beyaynetu platters in Addis Ababa to freshly roasted coffee corners across the country, great food brings people together. Ethio Review is your go-to community guide for exploring restaurants, reading honest feedback, and sharing your own dining experiences. Before you decide where to eat next, browse detailed restaurant profiles, check out must-try dishes, and see what fellow food lovers have to say. Just had an unforgettable meal—or a service experience worth talking about? Post a review on Ethio Review and help others find their next favorite spot.
