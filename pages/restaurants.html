Queen's Restaurant

A Provence-inspired dining destination featuring sweeping wooden arches, natural woven textures, and warm ambient lighting. Designed for relaxed yet upscale dining, it pairs a calm, nature-infused atmosphere with refined modern cuisine

Hearthstone Kitchen

A cozy, upscale dining room illuminated by woven rattan pendant lights and soft candlelight. Combining warm timber beams, stone pillars, and plush terracotta seating, it offers an intimate setting for evening dinners and craft drinks.

The Palm Patio

The Palm Patio is a relaxed outdoor lounge and grill with a clear glass ceiling and green palm trees. It has soft woven chairs, colorful floor tiles, and a bright neon wall sign. It is a great place to hang out with friends, enjoy grilled food, and spend a quiet afternoon or evening.
